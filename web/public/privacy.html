Educational Platform & Paper-Trading Notice

FinEx is strictly an educational financial simulation and learning platform designed to teach market mechanics, order types, risk profiling, and regulatory awareness. All trading on FinEx uses simulated virtual capital (₹1,00,000 virtual balance). FinEx is not a broker, does not connect to real demat accounts, does not hold customer funds or securities, and does not provide investment advisory services.

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how FinEx ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you visit https://www.finexai.tech (the "Site") or access the FinEx Terminal application.

We are committed to transparent data practices and data minimization. We only collect information that is strictly necessary to authenticate your access, deliver educational paper trading, maintain compliance records, and provide AI-assisted financial explanations.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Based on the actual technical implementation of FinEx, the platform processes the following categories of information:

A. Google Account Information (Google Sign-In / OAuth 2.0)

When you sign in using Google OAuth, Google Identity Services issues a cryptographically signed OpenID Connect ID token. Our backend verifies this token with Google's servers. From this token, we extract and store:

  • Unique Google Subject Identifier (sub): A persistent numeric identifier used to securely map your account across logins.
  • Email Address: Your primary Google account email, used as your unique login handle and for essential administrative notices.
  • Full Name: Your display name as provided by Google, used to personalize your workspace interface.
  • Profile Picture URL: If provided by your Google profile, used solely for displaying your avatar inside the terminal.

Note: We never receive, request, or store your Google account password, Google Drive files, contacts, or any other unrelated Google account data.

B. Simulated Paper Trading & Portfolio Data

To simulate order execution and portfolio management without financial risk, we store:

  • Your virtual cash balance (initialized at ₹1,00,000 virtual funds, with user-initiated resets supported).
  • Simulated orders placed (stock symbol, order side, quantity, order type, simulated price, fill status).
  • Paper portfolio holdings, average cost basis, realized/unrealized profit-and-loss calculations, and cash ledger records.

C. Educational Questionnaire & Preference Data

To illustrate how personal risk appetite shapes asset allocation:

  • Responses to our 4-question behavioral risk questionnaire and investment horizon selections, which compute an educational risk tier (Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive).
  • User-created market price alerts and watchlist selections.
  • Simulated goal targets and spending category summaries (processed if you choose to test the budgeting CSV upload feature).

D. AskFinEx AI Assistant Queries

Text prompts entered into the AskFinEx search and chat interface are processed to retrieve citations from official public regulatory corpora (SEBI Master Circulars, RBI Directions). Prompts are used exclusively to generate real-time educational responses and are not used to train proprietary public models.

E. Technical, Audit & Consent Records

To maintain operational security and compliance auditability:

  • Consent Records: Policy version acknowledged (e.g., v1.0), timestamp of consent, and client IP/user agent.
  • Session Management: Signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with 60-minute expiration, and a server-side revoked token blacklist to ensure instant revocation upon sign-out.
  • Error & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymized HTTP status codes, correlation IDs, and application events to maintain platform reliability.

5. Security & Session Protection

We implement strict security measures to protect user data and maintain service integrity:

  • Fail-Closed Authentication: All protected API endpoints verify cryptographic JWT tokens. Missing, malformed, expired, or revoked tokens return HTTP 401.
  • Session Expiration & Immediate Revocation: Access tokens expire after 60 minutes. When a user clicks Sign Out, the token is recorded in the server-side revocation list to prevent replay attacks.
  • Transport Encryption: All communications between the browser, Vercel frontend, Render backend, and database use TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • No Financial Asset Exposure: Because FinEx operates strictly with virtual funds and has no access to real bank or brokerage accounts, users face zero financial asset exposure.
