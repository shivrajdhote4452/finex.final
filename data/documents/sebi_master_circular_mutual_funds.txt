## Section 1: Portfolio Concentration Limits for Mutual Fund Schemes
Official Regulatory Publication: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026 (Circular No. HO/24/13/11(1)2026-IMD-POD-1/I/7602/2026, effective April 1, 2026, https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/mar-2026/1774024028162.pdf), originating in Clause 1 of the Seventh Schedule of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.

Applicable Regulatory Rule:
Under the SEBI portfolio concentration framework for mutual funds, a mutual fund scheme shall not invest more than 10% of its Net Asset Value (NAV) in equity shares or equity-related instruments of any single company.

Scope and Regulatory Exceptions:
1. Scheme-Level Application: This statutory 10% NAV single-issuer concentration limit applies at the mutual fund scheme level to actively managed mutual fund schemes to prevent concentration of unitholder capital in a single corporate issuer; under this circular, it does not govern individual personal direct equity portfolios.
2. Passive Index Funds and ETFs: Index Funds and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) that passively replicate benchmark indices are exempt from the 10% single-company limit to replicate index constituents under SEBI portfolio concentration norms (subject to index-level capping rules).
3. Sectoral and Thematic Funds: Sectoral and thematic mutual funds invest according to their designated investment mandates where sector concentration exceeds broad diversified limits.
4. Debt Instruments: Debt instruments are governed by distinct credit-rating-based single-issuer limits under the SEBI debt exposure framework.
