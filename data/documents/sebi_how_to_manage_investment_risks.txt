## Section 1: How to Manage Investment Risks
Page: 1
Asset allocation and portfolio diversification are foundational risk-mitigation principles in securities markets (Official Publication: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Investor Education, "How to Manage Investment Risks", https://investor.sebi.gov.in/investment_risk_managment.html).

Key Principles of Managing Investment Risk:
1. Asset Allocation: Distributing investments across distinct asset classes such as equity, debt, and cash equivalents based on individual investment horizon, financial goals, and risk profile. Equities offer potential for long-term inflation-beating growth but entail short-term volatility, whereas fixed income debt instruments offer stability and income while bearing purchasing-power risk.
2. Portfolio Diversification: Spreading capital across diverse securities, industries, sectors, and issuers. Diversification reduces unsystematic (company-specific and sector-specific) risk because adverse events impacting an individual company or sector do not affect the entire portfolio equally.
3. Prudent Risk Limitation: While asset allocation and diversification help manage and mitigate specific risks, diversification does not eliminate market risk (systematic risk) and does not guarantee protection against investment losses or assure positive returns during market downturns.
