## Section 1: Portfolio Diversification Heuristics for Retail Investors
Page: 1
FinEx Educational Summary: Practical Portfolio Diversification Heuristics.
While SEBI regulations do not prescribe statutory portfolio caps or percentage limits for individual retail investors in their personal direct equity holdings, financial planning practitioners and FinEx educational research commonly recommend the following illustrative heuristics to help reduce unsystematic risk:
1. Single-Stock Heuristic: Consider capping individual equity holdings under 10% to 15% of total equity to avoid disproportionate firm-specific downside.
2. Single-Sector Heuristic: Consider capping total exposure to a single industry under 25% to 30% of total equity holdings to mitigate sectoral downturns.
Regulatory & Risk Disclosures:
- These figures are illustrative FinEx educational summaries and financial planning heuristics; they are NOT statutory SEBI regulations for retail investors.
- Statutory 10% single-company concentration limits apply to actively managed mutual fund schemes under the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds (March 20, 2026) and Clause 1 of the Seventh Schedule of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996 (with exceptions for Index Funds and ETFs tracking benchmark indices); this statutory restriction does not apply to individual retail investors.
- Diversification helps reduce unsystematic (company-specific and sector-specific) risk, but does not eliminate systematic market risk and does not guarantee protection against investment losses.
