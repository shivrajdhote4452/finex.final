## Section 1: Portfolio Diversification Heuristics for Retail Investors
Page: 1
FinEx Educational Summary: Practical Portfolio Diversification Heuristics.
While the mutual fund 10% single-company concentration limit under the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds is a scheme-level restriction and does not govern individual personal direct equity portfolios, financial planning practitioners and FinEx educational research commonly suggest the following illustrative heuristics to help manage unsystematic risk:
1. Single-Stock Heuristic: Consider capping individual equity holdings under 10% to 15% of total portfolio equity to mitigate disproportionate firm-specific downside.
2. Single-Sector Heuristic: Consider capping total exposure to a single industry under 25% to 30% of total portfolio equity to manage sector-specific downturns.

Regulatory & Risk Disclosures:
- Clear Attribution: These percentage guidelines are illustrative FinEx educational summaries and financial planning heuristics; they are NOT statutory SEBI regulations for individual retail investors.
- Mutual Fund Concentration Norms: Statutory 10% single-company concentration limits apply to mutual fund schemes under the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026 (Circular No. HO/24/13/11(1)2026-IMD-POD-1/I/7602/2026, https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/mar-2026/1774024028162.pdf) and Clause 1 of the Seventh Schedule of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996 (with exceptions for Index Funds and ETFs tracking benchmark indices).
- Risk Limitation: Diversification can reduce unsystematic (company-specific and sector-specific) risk, but it does not eliminate market risk (systematic risk). It does NOT guarantee that other holdings will remain unaffected or offset losses, and it does not guarantee protection against investment losses.
